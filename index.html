01

Turn existing professional buyers into predictable reorders

Repeat revenueHigh-value path

What we found: Rewards pay 1 point per $1 and $5 per 100 points. CBG32 carries 10% off at 3+ and 20% off at 6+. SlimSpa also exposes a Buy Again page publicly.

Opportunity: No visible replenishment-focused creative, no reorder ad angle, and no strong “restock your room” pathway in the prospect-facing audit.

Commercial upside: SlimSpa already has the mechanics of a replenishment business. Our research shows the owner where repeat purchase is already being signaled — and where it still is not being sold.

02

Fix bilingual trust and policy parity

Trust riskSpanish market

What we found: The Spanish storefront promises free worldwide shipping, free 15-day returns and 24/7 support, while English policy pages present more limited terms. The audit confirms 8 policy conflicts.

Opportunity: Align every customer-facing promise across languages, policies and advertising — and make the Spanish buying journey as trustworthy as the English one.

Commercial upside: This is one of the fastest ways to make the audit feel like it understands the business at a trust-and-conversion level.
